YOUR RULES / 04

TRAIN SMART.
STAY CONSISTENT.

01

START LIGHT

For the first two weeks, learn the equipment and technique. Stop each set with around 2–3 good repetitions left.

02

TRACK YOUR LIFTS

Record the weight and reps you complete. When all sets feel controlled at the top of the rep range, increase weight a little.

03

RECOVER

Rest between hard sessions, sleep consistently, and take a rest day when you need one.

04

FUEL SAFELY

Eat and drink according to your needs. Because you've had gastric bypass surgery, confirm your protein target, hydration approach, vitamins, and lab monitoring with your bariatric team.

Safety note: this is general fitness guidance, not medical care. Follow any restrictions from your healthcare team. Stop if you feel faint, have chest pain, or experience significant or unusual pain.
